Key Takeaway

Everyone should participate in some type of mobility work within their exercise programming in order to maintain and improve their overall health. Yoga is a great option!

You don’t have to be flexible to participate in Yoga. Anyone can participate regardless of their current fitness level or ability. Adjustments can always be made so you get to make the Yoga class yours. Everyone is unique and what works for one, may not work for another. The focus is on your feeling, your body and your time for yourself.
