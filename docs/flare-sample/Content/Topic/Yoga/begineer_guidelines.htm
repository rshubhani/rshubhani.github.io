Beginner Guidelines & Safety

Yoga is personal so, avoid comparing yourself to others.

  • Always begin with a warm-up, move slowly, and pay attention to your breathing.

  • If you feel pain (not just a stretch), stop immediately.

  • Practicing in a quiet, uncluttered space will help you stay present and focused.

  • Start with 3–4 rounds, gradually increasing to 6–12.

  • Coordinate breath with movement (inhale when stretching upward or back, exhale when bending forward).

  • Move slowly and mindfully, avoiding jerky movements.
