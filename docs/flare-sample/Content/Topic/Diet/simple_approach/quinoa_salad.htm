Quinoa and Veggie Salad Recipe

A light, nutritious, protein-rich salad that can serve as lunch or dinner.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cooked quinoa

  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved

  • ½ cup cucumber, diced

  • ¼ cup red bell pepper, diced

  • 2 tbsp chopped fresh parsley or cilantro

  • 1 tbsp olive oil

  • 1 tbsp lemon juice

  • Salt and pepper to taste

Method

  1. Cook quinoa according to package instructions and let it cool slightly.

  2. In a large bowl, combine quinoa, tomatoes, cucumber, bell pepper, and parsley.

  3. Drizzle olive oil and lemon juice over the salad.

  4. Toss gently to mix. Season with salt and pepper.

  5. Serve fresh or chilled.

Benefits

Provides fiber, protein, and antioxidants. Easy to digest and energizing. For more information on this recipe, you can refer to Quinoa Vegetable Salad.
