The Patanjali - Inspired Approach to Eating

In yoga philosophy, foods are classified into three categories:

  • Sattvic (pure, light, balanced): fresh fruits, vegetables, legumes, whole grains, nuts, seeds, milk, and herbal teas. These foods promote peace, vitality, and clarity.

  • Rajasic (stimulating): spicy food, fried food, coffee, onion, garlic — they can overstimulate the mind, making it restless.

  • Tamasic (heavy, dulling): stale, processed food, alcohol, and overeating — these lead to lethargy and confusion.

A sattvic diet is central to Patanjali’s teachings, as it supports mental clarity, physical health, and spiritual progress.

If you want to read about Patanjali refer to Yoga Sutras of Patanjali for detailed information.
