Why Food Matters in Yoga

Yoga is more than just asanas and breathing exercises, it is a complete way of living. Patanjali, in the Yoga Sutras, emphasized purity (saucha), discipline (tapas), and moderation (mitahara) as essential elements of a yogic lifestyle. This means that what we eat is not separate from our practice, but deeply connected to it.

Food is energy, and the quality of that energy shapes not only the body but also the mind. A calm, steady mind and a healthy body are easier to cultivate when the diet supports clarity and balance. Just as yoga trains the body to be flexible and the mind to be steady, a mindful diet trains the digestive system to function smoothly and the nervous system to remain calm.
