Body and Fuel Connection

You have now explored the foundations of yoga through basic poses that strengthen the body and calm the mind. But just as movement nourishes the muscles and breath nourishes the lungs, the food we eat nourishes every cell.

Yoga and diet go hand in hand. A body that moves with ease and a mind that remains calm both depend on the quality of nourishment we provide. Let’s now turn to the principles of healthy eating and Patanjali’s diet philosophy, which teach us how to eat in a way that supports both wellness and inner peace.
