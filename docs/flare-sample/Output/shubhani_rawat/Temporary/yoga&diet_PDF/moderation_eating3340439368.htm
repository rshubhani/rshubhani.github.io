The Principle of Mitahara

Mitahara, or mindful moderation in eating, is a cornerstone of yogic nutrition. The idea is to eat enough to nourish but not so much that it overloads digestion. Yogis traditionally recommend filling the stomach with:

  • ½ food

  • ¼ water

  • ¼ left empty for ease of digestion

This practice prevents heaviness, improves energy, and supports meditation.

Fresh, Seasonal, and Simple

Nature provides what the body needs at the right time. Eating seasonal, local produce keeps digestion strong and supports immunity. A simple, home-cooked meal is preferred over complicated or processed dishes, as simplicity keeps the body light and the mind calm.
